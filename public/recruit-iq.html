Scoring categories

Excellent Match: 85–100% · Good Match: 70–84% · Moderate Match: 55–69% · Low Match: below 55%. Missing mandatory requirements are always flagged and do not automatically reject a candidate.

Default scoring weights

Skills 30% · Relevant experience 20% · Industry experience 15% · Role and responsibilities 15% · Education 10% · Tools and certifications 10%. Recruiters may edit weights for each job; all six values must total 100%.

Privacy and traceability

CV text and reports are encrypted at rest. Extracted profile fields are retained only when supported by text in the CV; mandatory evidence is checked against the CV text. AI is advisory and does not select or reject candidates. Candidate suitability must not use protected personal characteristics.

Document processing

Text-based PDF, DOC and DOCX extraction is supported. Scanned PDFs require pdftoppm and tesseract on the server (configurable with PDFTOPPM_CMD, TESSERACT_CMD, and optional OCR_LANG). AI extraction and matching require the configured server-side AI provider.

RecruitIQ supports human screening and is not an employment decision. Review source evidence and verify details directly with candidates. Contact information and CVs are sensitive personal data; limit access to authorized Talent Acquisition users.
